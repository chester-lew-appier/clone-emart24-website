What is e-kafe?

e-kafe is emart24’s own counter food and drinks category whereby the food and beverages are prepared fresh and adheres to JAKIM’s compliances and halal requirements.

With emart24’s internal processes and standard operating procedures already fine-tuned, we are primed to get five outlets certified halal each month and we aim to completely get all emart24’s e-kafe outlets certified by next year.

emart24 has implemented strict supplier vetting processes, thorough cleanliness protocols, and stringent quality controls. Suppliers are required to submit documentation reviewed by halal executives, with details uploaded to JAKIM’s official portal.

The certification process reinforces the company’s commitment to maintain the highest standards of compliance and transparency.
